# the floor under the volume convergence rung was measured, and it had never excluded a round

`escalate_on.new_findings_not_falling` ends a review cycle when the count of findings
no earlier round raised stops falling. `NOT_FALLING_MIN_NEW` is the only thing between
that rung and noise — both ends of the comparison have to be at least this many
findings — and it had never been calibrated. Its own comment said four was chosen to
match `FIX_INJECTION_MIN_NEW` next door, because two unmeasured floors differing by one
would be *"two things to defend and one of them would be defended by 'it is not the
other one'"*.

#710 asked whether the rung's own `1` should move now that `max_rounds` is 6 rather
than 2. Rich's answer of 2026-09-02 is that **`1` stays and the floor moves instead** —
the rung may still fire at round 2, but only on counts large enough to be a real trend.

### The measurement

Over the whole board, all time, on 2026-09-02:

```
curl -sH "Authorization: Bearer $QUARTERBACK_TOKEN" \
     "$QUARTERBACK_BASE_URL/reviews?limit=500"
```

115 runs, 90 of them in a cycle and reviewed, 42 cycles, **37 multi-round**. A cycle is
(repo, pr, cycle) and its series is each round's own `new_findings` — the same
population `GET /review/convergence`'s `marginal_by_round` publishes, so no new
instrument was needed.

* **Four had never excluded anything.** Of the 85 round counts recorded inside a cycle,
  none is below 4; the smallest is 7. The floor was not merely uncalibrated, it was
  inoperative.
* **Every firing the board can grade came out wrong.** A firing is gradeable when the
  cycle has a round recorded after it — 19 of the 22 firings at a floor of 4 do not, so
  the board says nothing about them. On all three that do (quarterback#161 16 → 25,
  lexray#1709 8 → 14, lexray#1697 10 → 13) the count came back down before the cycle
  ended.
* **17 is the smallest floor that clears all three**, set by the largest of them:
  #161 compares 16 and 25. #1709 is silent from 15 and #1697 from 11.
* **And the founding case still fires where the human stopped it.** quarterback#480 is
  44 → 38 → 41: round 2 falls and buys round 3, round 3 does not and ends the cycle. It
  keeps that at every floor up to 38, so the window is **[17, 38]** and 17 is its lower
  bound — the least intervention that clears the observed noise.

### The figure the old floor rested on did not reproduce

`harness_rules.py`, `panel_rounds.py`, `harness/loops/README.md` and the suite all
quoted the founding cycle as **44 → 15 → 18**. The board records quarterback#480's
cycle `a64ec1c4` as **44 → 38 → 41**, and no cycle anywhere on the board has a
44 → 15 → 18 series; the only 15 → 18 pair on it is quarterback#61's rounds 2 and 3,
eleven days earlier and a different cycle.

The correction is load-bearing rather than cosmetic. Read as 15 and 18, the founding
case caps the floor at 15 and conflicts with sparing #161's 16 → 25, which needs 17 —
and the honest answer would then have been a decision request naming the conflict. Read
as 38 and 41 there is no conflict. The argument for the *limit* of `1` is untouched by
it: a fall then a non-fall, ending where the human ended it, is the same shape at
different magnitudes.

### What the measurement cannot say

At 17 the rung fires on 13 of the 37 cycles (35%, down from 22 and 59%) and **all 13
firings land on the cycle's last recorded round**, so on this population it forgoes no
round anywhere — which also means it cannot be shown to be wrong and cannot be shown to
do anything. That is #637's finding arriving next door: the population was gathered
under `max_rounds: 2`, where round 2 was the round the cap was ending anyway, and
`converged` is TRUE on no cycle on this board, so there is no healthy cycle for a false
positive to have interrupted.

The one cycle started since `f53bdcd` raised the cap is lexray#1813 (7 → 13), which
fired at a floor of 4 and is silent at 17 — and 17 was **not** chosen to exclude it. A
floor of 8 would have done that, on n=1 and on a firing nothing can grade, which is
precisely the fitting #637 refused.

`NOT_FALLING_MIN_NEW` stays a **constant** and not a 29th dial (#621). Nothing measured
asks for it to be repo-specific: the three gradeable false positives span two
repositories and the founding case a third.
