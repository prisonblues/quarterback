The antigravity panel seat now takes its prompt on stdin, as one stream-json message, and reads its reply from the stream's final `result` event. Before, the prompt travelled in argv, where the kernel's 128 KiB cap truncated antigravity on large diffs. With no seat bound by argv, the per-seat clamp and the `argv_capped` coverage exemption are gone. A malformed or failed final result is now a seat failure with agy's own reason, even after an earlier success.
