# a class ledger keeps one round count, and an owner can continue a stopped cycle

On #841's cycle, the first with a class ledger, two things did not meet. The panel restarts
its round count on `--new-cycle` while the ledger does not, so sightings were recorded by hand
under ledger round numbers the panel never printed. And when the owner answered a
`fix_injection` stop with "run round 3", the panel refused: the only way through was
`--new-cycle`, which throws away the baseline and provenance the stop was measured from.

`panel.py` now takes the ledger's own next round for the budget check and the brief, and
writes it to the payload as `class_ledger.round` beside the panel's `cycle` and round. Every
`panel_classes.py` command takes `--payload <round.json>` for its round, PR and ledger, and
refuses flags that disagree with it. `panel_classes.py mark` records a clean round.

`panel.py --continue-cycle --answered-by NAME --why TEXT` runs the next round of a cycle a
terminal stop ended, with the same baseline and guards, and records the answer as
`stop_answered` in the payload and at the top of the posted summary. It does not lift the round
cap or the spend ceiling, and it is refused when there is no stop to answer.
