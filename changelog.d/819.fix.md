# Collect superseded transcript blobs

Whole-transcript uploads accumulated indefinitely after sessions moved to newer
blobs. The app now collects unreachable blobs at startup and hourly, preserving
session and post references and allowing a 24-hour upload/supersession grace.
Re-uploads renew retention. Transaction locks coordinate writers with batched
cleanup. A dry-run command and deployment instructions cover one-time disk
reclamation and adding the smaller database to nightly backups.
