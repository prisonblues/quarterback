# the round table shows what each fix pass wrote, and a ceiling can bound it

`prisonblues/lexray#1780`, five rounds. The fix passes after round 1 wrote 1,313
lines and 848 of them — 64.6% — were test and doc. No budget could see one of
them, and the one statistic that was watching went the wrong way.

### the statistic that went quiet

The panel prints **Guard-to-guarded** every round. Laid side by side, those five
lines read:

| round | source added | test added | doc added | guard ratio |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 476 | 883 | 170 | 2.21 |
| 2 | 653 (+177) | 1213 (+330) | 220 (+50) | 2.19 |
| 3 | 769 (+116) | 1418 (+205) | 220 | 2.13 |
| 4 | 883 (+114) | 1574 (+156) | 269 (+49) | 2.09 |
| 5 | 941 (+58) | 1632 (+58) | 269 | 2.02 |

Source nearly doubled. Test nearly doubled. **The ratio fell every round.** A
cumulative proportion cannot tell "this change is well guarded" from "this change
and its guards are both running away", because the runaway moves numerator and
denominator together. A `max_guard_ratio` would have fired on none of those five
rounds — and would fire at round 1 on a PR heavily guarded from the outset that
never churned at all. It measures the wrong thing in both directions, so
`guard_ratio` stays report-only and that is now a decision rather than a deferral.

Read as **deltas**, the same five rows say it plainly: rounds 2-5 wrote 380, 205,
205 and 58 lines of test and prose against 177, 116, 114 and 58 of production.

### `max_fix_guard_lines`, and it ships unset

A ceiling on the guard churn of **one fix pass**. It does not bank — each round
reads its own fix range and nothing earlier, so a quiet round cannot fund a loud
one, which is the case the ceiling is for. The count is
`panel_seats.referee_split`'s `test + prose` over that range: the same bucket
`unrefereed_line_weight` prices, mechanical, never a forecast and never the
fixer's judgement about its own work.

It defaults to `null`, and that is **unset** rather than off. The evidence is one
cycle; its quiet round wrote 58 guard lines and its loud one 380, and a threshold
drawn between them is a number chosen to fit one PR with its argument written
afterwards — which is exactly what #67 refuses. So every round takes the count and
publishes it (`round_stop.guard_churn`) and nothing fires until somebody writes a
number they can defend.

`escalate_on.guard_lines` decides what crossing it *does*, and is `false`. A repo
that writes a ceiling gets it measured and reported, and has to say so a second
time before a round ends on it: `max_fix_growth` ends a cycle on years of
measurement, this has one PR, and the weaker action goes first. Armed, it is
`round_stop`'s sixth rung and takes the same two bounds as the four beside it — it
can only turn a `go again` into a stop, and only the round rule 1 was buying.

### three columns in the round table

`round_stop.unrefereed_fix` has computed the production/test/prose split of every
fix pass since #554, and printed it in a line of its own — one round at a time,
which is the form that cannot be read *down*. It now sits in the cycle table
beside `introduced`:

```
round  findings  P1/P2  introduced  prod  test  prose  whole PR  vs r1
   r1         8      2           —     —     —      —   113,402  1.00x
   r2        14      5     9 (64%)   177   330     50   236,187  2.08x
   r3        15      4    13 (87%)   116   205      0   340,341  3.00x
```

A pass that wrote 330 test lines and a pass that wrote 330 production lines are
not the same event, and until now the table rendered them identically.
