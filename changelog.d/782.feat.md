# a cycle that attested to nothing is not a clean finish

"No finding" has two causes — nothing was wrong, and nothing ran — and the dry branch reported the
first without being able to tell them apart. A round 1 whose seats produced nothing anywhere
stopped, was confident, took no veto and held nothing outstanding, so it stamped `converged: true`
on the strength of a panel that never demonstrated it could see the diff.

`round_stop.attested` is `converged`'s fifth conjunct and is now stored beside it, published on
every run view and refused at the database when a round claims a clean finish it contradicts.
`GET /review/convergence` reports `unattested_cycles` alongside the rate: "this cycle did not
converge because a P1 was still outstanding" and "because the panel raised nothing at all" are
different facts with different repairs, and pooled into one `unconverged` count the rate says
neither.

The buckets themselves are deliberately unchanged. Whether an unattested cycle belongs in the
convergence denominator at all is a real question, and moving it would move a published rate as a
side effect of storing a field.
