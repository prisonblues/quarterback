# a finding has a life between rounds

A finding existed inside a round and nowhere else. It was raised, judged, maybe fixed, and what
became of it survived as prose in a report plus, when somebody remembered, one row saying what a
human decided. Nothing carried a finding's state from one round into the next, and the loop paid
for it three ways: a deferred finding came back next round looking like fresh damage, which lands
in `escalate_on.fix_injection` and stops cycles that were converging; a finding nobody was paid to
look at was indistinguishable from a finding nobody raised; and "did round 3's fix answer round 2's
complaint" needed a human reading two reports. No cycle this board has recorded has ever been
marked converged.

`POST /review/ledger` records where each finding stands at the end of a round and
`GET /review/ledger` reads a pull request's ledger back at the start of the next one. Every row is
stamped with the round it happened in, the writer that wrote it, and the reason it got there.

**Named skip reasons, not one bucket.** The five recorded outcomes — `fixed`, `narrowed`,
`refuted`, `deferred`, `superseded` — are extended, not paralleled, with the three an outcome
cannot express because none of them is a decision: `raised`, `unpaid` (a budget ran out before
anybody looked) and `escalated`. `unpaid` beside `deferred` is the point: "nobody looked" and
"somebody looked and said no" argue for different repairs, and only one of them is fixed by raising
a budget.

**A promotion keeps the finding's original round.** A finding deferred in round 2 and returned in
round 5 reads as two rounds old, not as damage round 5 caused. Returning it is the one move that
costs a reason, and returning it any other way is refused — coming back is fine, coming back
silently is what the ledger was built to end.

The board stores whatever identity the harness sends and computes none: findings are matched across
rounds by diff-hunk overlap in `harness/loops/panel_locality.py`, and a board-side identity would be
a second opinion formed without the diff.
