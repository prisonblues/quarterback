# a repeat is where a finding landed, and the board counts whether that was worth it

Cross-round finding identity used to be a hash of the reviewing model's own wording, so the same defect restated in different words on the next round was a new finding: it bought a round it had already bought, it landed in `escalate_on.fix_injection` as fresh damage, and it never aged. Findings are now matched by locality as well — the line range against the round's diff hunks, within a slack — and the panel passes the union of the two matchers on.

Which makes the change invisible from the outside. Every downstream number is computed off that union, so a locality matcher that caught nothing and one that caught half a cycle's repeats produce an identical row. `review_runs.repeats_only_locality` and `repeats_by_key` are what tell them apart: how many of a round's repeats only the locality matcher recognised, and the population the wording hash already had. Both ride every run view and `GET /review/findings`, and `GET /review/convergence` reports the fleet-wide `locality_repeats` share beside the rate.

Two integers per run, and stored rather than left in the payload for the reason the counts exist at all: the verdict this feature is owed is a query over hundreds of rounds across every PR the fleet reviews, and a round's published JSON lives in a temp directory on whichever host ran the panel. A fleet where the share stays near zero is a fleet where the matcher should come out — a decision that would otherwise be made on somebody's recollection of a report.

NULL is "the panel did not say" and never zero, which is the opposite direction from `attested` and matters just as much: "the locality matcher caught nothing extra" is the finding the columns exist to report, and a backfilled zero would build the case against the matcher out of rounds that never ran it.

`keys` and `why` ride the same block and deliberately get no column — per-round detail already in the payload a reader can fetch, and `len(keys)` is `only_locality` by construction. The convergence buckets are untouched: `CYCLE_ENDINGS` is what the rate divides and #637's recalibration is measured against exactly that number.
