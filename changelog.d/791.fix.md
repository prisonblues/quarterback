# the round built around withholding the diff no longer tells its seats the diff follows

The PR claim block closes with a fence, and the fence is a promise about what comes next:
`--- END OF THE AUTHOR'S CLAIM — THE DIFF (THE EVIDENCE) FOLLOWS ---`. On a move-manifest
round nothing follows it but the manifest. That round is the one prompt in the panel built
around withholding the diff, and it was the one telling every seat the diff was coming.

Not a wording slip. The claim block's own framing exists to stop a seat answering from the
author's assertion instead of from the evidence — and a seat told the evidence follows and
then handed none is being invited to do exactly that, with the claim as the only thing in
front of it. It is #628's shape: the remedy is to make the sentence true, not to append a
warning to a sentence that is not.

The frame and the fence are now selected as a **pair**, keyed on the same verdict that
selects the brief and swaps the material, so the opening promise and the closing fence
cannot be chosen independently — which is what this defect would become if somebody fixed
only one of them. A manifest round gets a fence naming the manifest, and a paragraph saying
what a manifest can actually settle: the *shape* of the move, which is what a PR asserting
"this only moves X into Y" is claiming. A claim about what the moved code now **does** is
routed to the `unverifiable claim nobody has checked` grade the brief already carries,
rather than being accepted because it sounds right.

The block is kept and corrected rather than dropped from manifest rounds, which is the
cheaper fix and was the wrong one twice over: a manifest genuinely settles a shape claim, and
dropping the block would have removed this round from the on/off comparison that measures
whether priming a seat with the claim helps at all.

Fence neutralisation now walks **all three** marks rather than the two a round renders. An
author cannot know which variant the verdict will pick, so a body forging the other round's
fence is the same forged-structure attack with a different string.
