# a finding now says whether the last fix pass wrote the lines it lands on

The panel already worked this out one finding at a time and then threw it away. `_provenance`
decides, for every new outstanding finding, whether the fix pass immediately before it wrote the
lines the finding sits on — and the only thing that survived was the pool: `fix_injection`, one
rate per round, which either ends the cycle or does not. So a fixer reading its brief could not
tell which of the findings in front of it were about its own last pass, and a round could not tell
a repo answering one edit with five prose findings apart from a loop circling. lexray#1611 round 2
is what that cost: thirteen new findings, nine attributed, 69%, cycle stopped — and six of the
thirteen were about *text*, four of them stale precisely because that round had rewritten the thing
they described.

**Every finding in the report carries its own verdict.** 🔧 the last pass wrote those lines, 🔍
they were already there and the last round did not catch them. The sentence explaining what the
marks mean is printed once, above the list, so a row costs a marker and nothing more — a clause
repeated on every finding would take more width than the titles do. A finding nothing could
attribute gets no mark at all, deliberately: a mark on an unattributable finding is a claim about
the fix pass, and a false one. The same rows ride the payload at
`round_stop.fix_injection.findings`, each with the file that was classified beside the
classification, so a reader can check the judgement rather than take it.

**And the rate is split by what the finding is about** — production code, test, or prose — at
`round_stop.fix_injection.by_kind`, with a line in the report beside the pooled figure. Every kind
appears on every round: a bucket that is absent and a bucket with no findings in it are different
claims, and a consumer joining the three against the pooled denominator has to be able to see that
they add up.

**The pooled rate is deliberately unchanged, and that is a constraint rather than an omission.**
`escalate_on.fix_injection` still gates on exactly the number it has always gated on. Its `0.5` is
being recalibrated (#637) against that denominator, and no cycle carrying an attributable round has
ever been recorded as converged — 0 of 27, with 128 of 201 new findings across seven PRs written by
the fix pass immediately before them — so the population that would justify moving it does not
exist yet. Moving the denominator underneath a threshold while somebody is measuring it would
invalidate the measurement. The split is therefore published beside the rate and nothing reads it,
on this repo's instrument-before-gate rule: what arms it is a few dozen cycles of the by-kind
numbers accumulating, and then a threshold argued from them rather than from one PR.
